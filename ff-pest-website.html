FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pest control services.

How long does a pest treatment take? +
Most standard residential treatments take between 1 to 3 hours depending on property size and the type of pest being treated. Larger commercial properties or severe infestations may require more time. Our technician will give you a clear time estimate before starting.
Are your chemicals safe for children and pets? +
Yes. All chemicals we use are government-approved and professionally formulated to be safe for humans and animals once dry — usually within 2 to 4 hours. We recommend keeping children and pets away from treated areas until fully dry and ventilated.
Do you operate across all provinces? +
Yes. FF Pest & Weeds Control offers nationwide service across all 9 provinces of South Africa, including Gauteng, Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, Eastern Cape, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, Free State, North West, and Northern Cape.
How do I get a quote? +
Getting a quote is easy and free. You can use the contact form on this page, call us directly at 073 077 3295, or WhatsApp us at the link provided. We typically respond within 1 hour during business hours.
How often should I schedule pest control? +
For residential properties, we recommend a general pest treatment every 3 to 6 months for prevention. Properties with active infestations, food businesses, or warehouses may benefit from monthly or bi-monthly treatments. We will advise the best schedule for your specific situation.
What if the pests come back after treatment? +
We offer a guarantee on our treatments. If you experience a re-infestation of the same pest within the guaranteed period after treatment, we will return and re-treat at no additional charge. Our goal is your lasting satisfaction and a truly pest-free environment.
